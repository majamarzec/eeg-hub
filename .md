# 🧬 cellular and biophysical foundations of EEG

## central questions
* How do neural mechanisms responsible for generating scalp potentials work?
* How are they recorded by the specialized electronic differential micro-voltmeters with buit-in amplitude amplifiers?


💡 The in-depth understanding of [electrodynamics](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1hZI57JyUvYTF5POhjJJi4DXI12t16hBa?usp=share_link) of neural mechanisms requires academic-level knowledge on Maxwell's laws, volumetric conduction through isotropic/ anisotropic matrices (Poisson's equation), boundary conditions, electrostatics, multipole expansion.
